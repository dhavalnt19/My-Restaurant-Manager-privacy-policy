# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 2026

My Restaurant Manager ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects information.

## Information We Collect

The App may collect and store the following information:

* Restaurant information entered by users
* Menu items, orders, billing records, and sales data
* Device information required for app functionality
* Application logs and crash reports for troubleshooting

## How We Use Information

The information collected is used to:

* Provide restaurant management functionality
* Generate bills and sales reports
* Improve app performance and stability
* Diagnose technical issues

## Data Storage

Data entered into the App may be stored locally on the user's device and/or on secure cloud services used by the App.

We take reasonable measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

## Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies, including:

* Google Play Services
* Google AdMob (if advertisements are enabled)
* Firebase Analytics and Crash Reporting (if enabled)

## Advertising

The current version of the App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. These advertising services may collect device identifiers and usage information to serve relevant ads.

Users can review Google's privacy practices at:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may only be shared:

* When required by law
* To protect legal rights
* With service providers necessary for operating the App

## Children's Privacy

The App is intended for business and restaurant management purposes and is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.
